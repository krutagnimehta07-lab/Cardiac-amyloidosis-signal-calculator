Methods, definitions & limitations

Cohort and outcome

National Inpatient Sample heart-failure discharges within the ATTR-wt coding domain, meaning discharges from the first quarter in which ICD-10-CM E85.82 is observed (2017 Q4) onward. The outcome is a coded diagnosis of wild-type transthyretin amyloidosis (E85.82).

Model

Integer points were derived from a weighted logistic model after SHAP-guided variable selection with clinician-forced additions. Score features: age, sex, atrial fibrillation/flutter, chronic kidney disease, pacemaker, unspecified hypotension, spinal stenosis, bundle/fascicular block and hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. Score bands are quantile-style groupings of the score with their weighted observed ATTR-wt rates.

Reading the output

  • The observed rate is the proportion of discharges in that score band coded with ATTR-wt, not an individual's probability of disease.
  • Scores below 5 are shown in band 1 and scores above 47 in band 5, the ranges observed in the data.

Limitations

  • Administrative codes only: no echocardiography, cardiac MRI, scintigraphy or genotyping. ATTR-wt is under-diagnosed, so observed rates understate true prevalence.
  • Coded hypertrophic cardiomyopathy carries 13 points. This likely reflects infiltrative wall thickening coded as hypertrophic cardiomyopathy before or during the amyloid work-up, and it should not be read as primary hypertrophic cardiomyopathy predicting ATTR-wt.
  • Diagnoses may be coded anywhere in the stay, so some features could be consequences of the work-up rather than antecedents.
  • Not externally validated. For research and demonstration only.
